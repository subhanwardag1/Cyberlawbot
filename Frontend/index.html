About

What is CyberLaw Bot?

An AI assistant that answers questions about Pakistani criminal law — the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), Pakistan Penal Code (PPC), PECA 2016, and the PECA 2025 Amendment — in English and Urdu.

How it works (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

  1. Your question is converted into a numerical embedding.
  2. The most relevant statute sections are retrieved from a custom vector index of 2,373 indexed provisions.
  3. Gemini writes an answer grounded only in those sections and cites them.

Every answer shows the exact sources it used, so you can verify each claim.

Tech stack

FastAPI backend · Google Gemini (embedding + generation) · a custom NumPy cosine-similarity vector search. No LangChain and no external vector database — the retrieval engine is hand-built. Typical response time is under 5 seconds.

Important

CyberLaw Bot provides legal information, not legal advice, and does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Always confirm with a qualified lawyer and official sources before acting.

Your privacy

  • No accounts, no login, no personal data collection.
  • No PII retention. Conversations live in server memory for the current session only and are never written to a database. They disappear on “New Chat” or when the server restarts.
  • No cookies, trackers, or analytics.
  • Feedback is anonymous. If you rate an answer, we store only the vote, a timestamp, and a one-way hash of the question — never the raw text and never your identity.

Third parties

Your question is sent to Google Gemini to generate the answer, subject to Google's terms. We do not share data with any other third party.

Please do not share

Do not enter anyone's private or identifying information (names, CNIC numbers, phone numbers, addresses). Ask about the law in general terms.

Terms of use
